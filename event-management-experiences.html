03 // Our Event Planning Process

Our Event
Planning Process.

A structured five-step methodology uniting concept incubation, spatial styling, vendor coordination, and seamless live execution.

01

Discover & Define

We begin by understanding your brand, event objectives, target audience, budget and expected outcomes. This establishes the foundation for a relevant and practical event concept.

02

Conceptualise & Plan

We develop the creative direction and outline the event format, guest journey, key touchpoints and production requirements. We also establish a project timeline and define responsibilities.

03

Design & Coordinate

We refine the event's visual identity and coordinate the agreed requirements, which may include venue arrangements, vendors, branded materials, technical needs and promotional assets.

04

Produce & Execute

We coordinate the planned activities according to the agreed schedule and responsibilities. The focus is on bringing the concept to life while managing the details that contribute to a cohesive guest experience.

05

Review & Reflect

After the event, we review relevant outcomes against the original objectives. Depending on the event, this may include attendance, audience engagement, feedback, content generated and other agreed indicators.
