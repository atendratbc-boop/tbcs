03 // Methodology

Our Website Design &
Development Process.

A structured five-step approach ensuring clear collaboration, architectural rigor, and smooth delivery.

01

Discover & Strategise

We learn about your business, audience, objectives and brand identity. This helps us define the website's purpose, required features and overall creative direction.

02

Plan & Structure

We organise the sitemap, page hierarchy, content flow and key user journeys. A clear structure gives your website a strong foundation and makes information easier to navigate.

03

Design & Refine

We develop website layouts and visual concepts that align with your brand. We refine typography, colours, imagery and interface elements to create a cohesive digital experience.

04

Develop & Integrate

Once the design is approved, we build the website and configure the required functionality. Depending on the project, this may include a content management system, Shopify features, contact forms and third-party integrations.

05

Test & Launch

Before launch, we review the website across relevant screen sizes and browsers, check key interactions and address identified issues. We also consider essential on-page SEO elements and technical basics to help establish a sound foundation for future growth.
